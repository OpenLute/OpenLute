# OpenLute Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

OpenLute is intended to be an open, welcoming, and constructive community for builders, musicians, designers, makers, and anyone interested in developing open-source lute designs.

We ask everyone participating in the project to treat one another with respect and to contribute in a way that helps the project and its community improve.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful of differing opinions and approaches
* Giving and accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing discussions on the design, the problem, and the project rather than individuals
* Sharing knowledge and experience
* Giving appropriate credit to other contributors
* Being patient with people who have different levels of experience
* Helping create an environment where people feel comfortable asking questions and proposing ideas

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment or personal attacks
* Threats or intimidation
* Discriminatory or hateful behavior
* Deliberate disruption of project discussions or collaboration
* Publishing someone's private information without permission
* Repeatedly directing unwanted personal criticism toward another participant
* Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or collaborative community

## Technical Disagreement

OpenLute is an engineering and design project. Strong disagreement about technical decisions is expected and can be productive.

Criticism of a design, proposal, implementation, or engineering decision is welcome.

Criticism should remain focused on the work rather than becoming personal.

The goal is not to eliminate disagreement, but to make disagreement useful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within OpenLute's official project spaces, including GitHub Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, and other spaces operated on behalf of the project.

It also applies when someone is representing OpenLute in another public setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing violations of this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may:

* Ask participants to modify or stop inappropriate behavior
* Remove inappropriate comments or contributions
* Close discussions or issues
* Restrict participation in project spaces
* Temporarily or permanently ban participation in the project

Serious or repeated violations may result in immediate removal from project spaces.

Reports should be made privately to the OpenLute project maintainers rather than through a public issue or discussion.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Good Faith

The Code of Conduct should be applied in good faith and with reasonable judgment.

The existence of disagreement, criticism, or an unpopular opinion does not by itself constitute a violation.

OpenLute is intended to encourage open technical discussion while maintaining a respectful environment for everyone involved.
