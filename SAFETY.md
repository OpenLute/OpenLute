# Safety

OpenLute designs are open-source hardware provided under the **CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 – Strongly Reciprocal (CERN-OHL-S-2.0)**.

The license provides the designs without warranties and limits liability to the extent permitted by applicable law. **Build and use OpenLute designs at your own risk.**

When building or using an OpenLute, use appropriate safety practices for:

* 3D printing and fabrication equipment
* Hand and power tools
* Adhesives and other materials
* Hardware and structural components
* Strings and other tensioned components

Inspect components before use and do not use parts that are cracked, damaged, improperly manufactured, or otherwise appear unsafe.

When installing and tensioning strings, increase tension gradually and use appropriate caution. Stop if you observe cracking, deformation, unexpected movement, or other signs of failure.

Modifying a design, changing materials, changing print settings, or substituting hardware may affect the strength and behavior of the instrument.

**Use common sense, follow appropriate safety practices, and build responsibly.**
